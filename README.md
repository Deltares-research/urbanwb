Basic water balance tool
The water balance tool is developed in EXCEL for application in urban area. 
The water balance tool describes all possible urban water flows and the associated water resources. 
Rainfall-runoff, shallow groundwater (saturated and unsaturated), surface water and sewage (mixed and separated) are included. 
Water exchanges are included with atmosphere, deep groundwater, outside water and waste water treatment plant.
Basic time step is 1 day. That way time series of more than 100 year rainfall and evaporation can be managed. 
A smaller time step can be applied (1 hour, 15 minutes, 5 minutes). However a smaller time step implies a shorter time series (in EXCEL). 
Transforming the water balance tool to a script (e.g. Python) may solve this limitation. 
In addition a smaller time step requires some different boundary conditions.
